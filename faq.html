Frequently Asked Questions

Essential clarifications on architectural commissions, cinematic 3D CGI visualization workflows, technical deliverables, and bespoke brand identity design with KB CREATIVE.

We specialize in three integrated disciplines: (1) Spatial Architecture for residential villas (such as the Kara G+2 Residence), interior workplace renovations (Acute Engineering), and commercial mixed-use concourses (MENA MALL); (2) Cinematic 3D CGI Visualization featuring photorealistic atmospheric daylight simulations, materiality texturing, and BIM documentation in Autodesk Revit & D5 Render; and (3) Corporate Brand Identity Systems covering logomarks, typography, and packaging architecture.

Every project follows an architectural 4-stage methodology: (1) Brief & Site Analysis: clarifying programmatic requirements, plot dimensions, slope, and solar angles; (2) Spatial Massing & Circulation Zoning: structural grid layout and functional space hierarchy; (3) Photorealistic 3D Simulation: exploring lighting conditions, materials, and camera perspectives for client alignment; and (4) Technical Working Drawings: dimensioned architectural plans, sections, joinery specifications, and high-resolution marketing visuals.

To initiate a 3D visualization commission, you can provide CAD files (.dwg), 3D models (Revit, Rhino, SketchUp, or Blender), architectural PDF drawings, or even hand-sketched floor plans with dimensions. Providing reference imagery, landscape context, or preferred material palettes accelerates the lighting and texturing phase.

Yes. A core strength of KB CREATIVE is the cross-disciplinary union of spatial architecture and corporate visual identity. For commercial developments, retail malls (e.g., MENA MALL), and corporate offices (Acute Engineering), we design the architectural spaces alongside the 3D illuminated exterior storefront signage, interior wayfinding graphics, and company branding collateral.

Every brand system commissioned with KB CREATIVE includes: (1) Primary & Secondary Logomarks, geometric vector monograms, and responsive icon variations; (2) Brand Architecture Guidelines (clearspace rules, chromatic codes in CMYK/RGB/HEX, typographic hierarchy); (3) Stationery & Executive Collateral (business cards, letterheads, invoice templates); and (4) Real-World Mockups illustrating storefront signage, apparel embroidery, and digital UI/UX implementations.

We believe in structured milestone collaboration. Each commission includes dedicated review rounds at pivotal checkpoints (e.g. massing approval, lighting/material tests, and final presentation). This ensures full client alignment at every stage before technical documentation or final production assets are exported.
